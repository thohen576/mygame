Blind Ranking Britain

You get ten British things, one at a time. Put each in a slot from 1 (best) to 10 (worst) before you see the next one. No take-backs.

Photos are loaded from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons when you start a round. If a photo can't load, you'll see a name card instead and the game still works.
